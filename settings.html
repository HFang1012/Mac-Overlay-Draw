Scrawl

Hold a key.
Draw on anything.

Hold the wheel key and a wheel opens at your cursor. Slide toward a tool and let go to pick it. Let go in the middle to hide or show drawings. With a tool in hand, drag or hold the draw key to draw, and use ⌘Z or ⌘⇧Z to undo or redo. Pick the tool again, press Esc, or right-click to put it down.
