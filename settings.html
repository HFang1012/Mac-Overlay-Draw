Scrawl

Hold a key.
Draw on anything.

Hold the wheel key and a wheel opens at your cursor. Slide toward a tool and let go to pick it, or let go in the middle to cancel. Point at the pencil to pick a color too. With a tool in hand, drag or hold the draw key to draw, and use ⌘Z or ⌘⇧Z to undo or redo. Pick the tool again, press Esc, or right-click to put it down.

Away

Step away.
Leave a note.

Press the away keys and every screen blurs behind soft, drifting color. Your pointer hides and your note sits in the middle, so anyone walking by knows you’ll be right back. Press the same keys when you return.
